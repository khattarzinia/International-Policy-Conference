IPC Chapters

We are building IPC chapters at leading high schools and universities worldwide. Chapter leaders coordinate local outreach, organize information sessions, and support participants through the competition process.

01 · Local Outreach
Introduce IPC to policy clubs, debate societies, and interested students across your campus.
02 · Info Sessions
Host briefings walking prospective participants through categories, timelines, and brief standards.
03 · Team Support
Connect students forming teams and keep local competitors on track through submission deadlines.
